Frequently Asked Questions

Our Lead Management Software is a centralized tool designed to help developers, brokers, and tenants streamline their lead tracking and deal management processes. It provides features for lead scoring, collaboration, and deal stage customization to ensure that no opportunity is missed.

The platform is designed for:
  • Brokers: To track proposals, collaborate on deals, and manage leads in real time.
  • Developers: To analyze lead performance, manage deal stages, and optimize strategies for higher closure rates.
  • Tenants: To access and review proposals and LOIs (Letters of Intent) at any stage of negotiation.

Lead scoring allows you to categorize leads as Hot, Cold, or Warm, giving a clear action plan based on the likelihood of closing the deal. Each lead can also have a probability of success assigned, helping prioritize efforts.

The software enables task and comment management, allowing team members to assign tasks, leave comments, and collaborate in real time. This enhances coordination and ensures that everyone is aligned in the deal-making process.

Yes, the platform offers fully customizable deal stages. You can define sub-stages, and easily mark deals as active or inactive, creating a clear path for tracking the progression of each lead from start to close.

The holistic view feature provides a complete overview of the entire lead flow. It allows you to track each deal from the beginning to the end, offering a transparent view of deal progression and helping you monitor where opportunities stand.

Brokers can:
  • Seamlessly collaborate on deals with their team.
  • Track proposals and LOIs (Letters of Intent) in real time.
  • Customize proposals and preset LOI fields.
  • Save and email proposals directly from the app, reducing time spent on administrative tasks.

Developers can:
  • Track all leads and proposals in one centralized platform.
  • Manage and customize deal stages for better organization.
  • Analyze the probability of success to refine strategies.
  • Use advanced reporting tools to improve deal closure rates.

Tenants can:
  • Access proposals and LOIs at any stage of negotiation.
  • Review communications and versions, as all are stored in one place for easy reference.
  • Benefit from a streamlined negotiation process, with fewer administrative hurdles.
